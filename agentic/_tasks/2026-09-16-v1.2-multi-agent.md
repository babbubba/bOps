Operator decisions: `agentic/06-decisions.md` D-026

## Outcome

Delegate one objective among logical specialized agents in one process with explicit identity,
strictly reduced privileges, bounded resources, persistence and complete audit correlation. The
pipeline is fixed and deterministic: Discovery, Diagnostic, human approval of the plan hash,
Remediation, independent Verification. Agents never approve. Parallel execution, agent-to-agent
conversation, per-role models and any remote or Control Plane behavior are out of scope.
