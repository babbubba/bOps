# V1.2-E — nested budgets, deadlines and cancellation

Status: **planned**
Recommended model effort: **alto**
Repository: `bOps`
Depends on: V1.2-D complete
Governing ADR: `docs/architecture/adr/0030-privilege-reducing-multi-agent-delegation.md`

## Outcome

Make budgets, deadlines and cancellation hold across nested work (ADR-0030 section 6).

## Checklist

- [ ] Failing tests first: exhausted step/token budget, deadline expiry, cancellation during each role and during a side-effecting step, cancellation while awaiting approval.
- [ ] Child budget and deadline are reserved from the parent's remaining amount and reconciled at role end.
- [ ] Count a role's steps and tokens and stop at its deadline. V1.2-C2 deliberately leaves them out of the step path: the envelope check in `ExecuteStepAsync` covers tool, risk, window and Skill scope, and using up a granted budget or reaching the deadline is `BudgetExceeded` or `DeadlineExceeded`, not a denial of authority (ADR-0031 §3). The delegated entry points do not yet enforce them.
- [ ] One cancellation token tree covers run, role and step; each end state is a distinct audited terminal state.
- [ ] A cancelled side-effecting step with an unknown outcome is recorded as unknown, not as failed.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Budgets, deadlines and cancellation are proven across nested work.
- [ ] Restarting a role never resets its budget.
