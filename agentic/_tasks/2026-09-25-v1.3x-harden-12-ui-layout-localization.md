## Objective

Every primary UI surface is visually stable in English, Italian and longer languages at mobile, tablet,
1024 px-class and large desktop widths, with accessible full labels whenever text is clipped — without
shortening translations to hide layout defects.

## Confirmed defects addressed

**C-18**, measured (plan §2.6):
- Italian, 320 px Dashboard column: header needs 365 px, has 286 px; the status `<select>` (222 px, sized
  to "Limite di repianificazioni raggiunto") overflows the card by **78 px**; **110 px** at 288 px.
  English overflows by 22 px at 288 px.
- `StatusBadge` Italian labels wrap to 3 lines (52 px) inside a `rounded-full` pill.
- Mobile nav, Italian, 375 px: 472 px of content, **97 px** horizontal page overflow.
- English status labels are raw enum names (`MaxStepsReached`, `ReplanLimitReached`, …; `en.ts:52-55`).
- Detail header title/id block lacks `min-w-0` (long goal or UUID overflows on mobile).

## Source finding IDs

F-20.

## Scope

1. Dashboard list header: `flex-wrap`; status filter on its own row or `min-w-0 w-full max-w-[14rem]`,
   with a visible `<label>` (keep `aria-label`).
2. `StatusBadge`: `shrink-0 whitespace-nowrap max-w-[12rem] truncate`, `title` and `aria-label` with the
   full label; list row keeps `min-w-0` on the goal.
3. App nav below `md`: `overflow-x-auto` (or `flex-wrap`) with visible focus.
4. Detail header: `min-w-0 break-words`; `break-all` for ids.
5. Same audit and fixes for Delegations (status pill, list), Approvals, Plugins (5 pills, 2 selects),
   Settings, Login.
6. Human-readable English status labels.
7. A +40 % pseudo-locale for tests only (not shipped in the language switch).
8. Automated overflow assertions: for every card/section, `scrollWidth <= clientWidth` at 375, 768, 1024
   and 1440 px in `en`, `it` and the pseudo-locale.

## Out of scope

- Shortening Italian strings to fit.
- Behaviour changes (HARDEN-4, HARDEN-10, HARDEN-11 own them).
- Visual redesign.

## Architecture constraints

Accessibility is not sacrificed to hide overflow: clipped text always has an accessible full label;
focus order and contrast unchanged or improved.
