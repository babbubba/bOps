## Objective

A long troubleshooting session keeps a bounded prompt size and a bounded cost while the complete
evidence record stays persisted and retrievable. Budgets are safe by default.

## Confirmed defects addressed

- About 47 KB of each 58 KB request in task 88f97dda is the 100 tool schemas (≈ 11–12 K prompt tokens
  per call; 12,832–19,797 prompt tokens per call with few messages).
- Each observation is truncated to `MaxObservationCharacters=4000` independently, but history
  accumulates all of them; the replan prompt ("Steps taken so far") grows by up to 4,000 characters per
  step (9,283 characters at step 6).
- `MaxTotalTokens` defaults to `null`: no token/cost budget at all by default.
- No handling for context overflow.

### Additional real-world evidence (2026-10-03; HARDEN-9 PR #74 regression)

A diagnostic of months of Windows freezes took **12 steps, 269,452 tokens and 0 replans**. Even after per-result
shortening, `system.stability` contributed about **7,066** characters, `system.crashes` about **10,966**, and
`service.list` about **6,597** before shortening. The bounded observations remained repeatedly expensive in model
history. This observation strengthens the existing compaction and typed-evidence projection motivation; it changes no
HARDEN-8 implementation or acceptance criterion.

## Source finding IDs

F-18, F-07 (budget defaults). Uses `ContextOverflow` from HARDEN-2 and attempts/cumulative tokens from

## Scope

1. **Bounded history**: the last K completed tool-call steps remain verbatim (default 3), the next
   M=12 older steps use runtime-authored records of at most 256 UTF-16 code units each, and all
   earlier steps use one typed archive summary of at most 512 code units. Fixed history headers
   have a 128-unit cap. No raw historical evidence enters either representation; persisted
   `TaskState` and audit remain complete.
2. **Replan digest**: `ReplanAsync` uses the compacted digest instead of re-sending every truncated
   observation.
3. **Evidence retrieval**: the exact internal `EvidenceRead/v1` text directive names
   `evidenceId`, explicit `source=result|observation`, `offset`, and `length`. Each range is at
   most 4,000 UTF-16 code units; four requests per logical step or replan. Every attempted read
   is metadata-audited. No operational tool or generic datastore accessor is introduced.
4. **Context overflow**: a `ModelFailureKind.ContextOverflow` with compactable history retries
   once in aggressive K=0 mode; a call without such history, including an initial plan, ends
   without an identical retry. A second overflow ends the logical call.
5. **Default budgets**: ship `Agent:MaxTotalTokens=350000` cumulative over every attempt and
   `Agent:MaxAttemptDuration=01:00:00` per attempt; retain HARDEN-3's step/replan lifetime limits
   and task-view accounting. Human approval waiting is excluded from active attempt time.
   Additive Abstractions contracts are `TaskTerminalKind.AttemptDurationBudget`,
   `PlanStep.VerificationStatus`, and metadata-only `EvidenceReadAuditEvent`.

## Inputs from the HARDEN-9 architecture gate (2026-10-02, revised 2026-10-03)

Interface only; HARDEN-9 implements none of this ([ADR-0042](../../docs/architecture/adr/0042-evidence-reasoning-and-limitation-disclosure.md) §16, accepted 2026-10-03):

- The HARDEN-9 evidence-limitations digest is built from **persisted** steps and must stay unaffected by compaction; a
  compacted step is not an evidence limitation by itself.
- The digest references steps by **step index**; evidence ids introduced here must resolve to it (or the ADR-0014
  amendment moves the digest to evidence ids).
- The HARDEN-9 evidence rule (≤ 2,000 characters, every model call) and the digest (≤ 4,608 characters, step calls) are
  fixed prompt costs the budget must count.
- **Observation budget vs typed evidence.** `MaxObservationCharacters` (4,000 by default) is far below the outputs of
  `system.events` (default 32,768 bytes), `system.stability` (32,768) and `system.crashes` (65,536), so the model sees only
  head and tail fragments of them. HARDEN-9 detects and discloses it per step and does not change the budget; the
  context-budget economy and the full typed-evidence projection strategy belong here — retrieval "bounded by
  `MaxObservationCharacters`" alone cannot reach the cut part of a single observation, so the ADR-0014 amendment must
  address it (for example a per-observation budget that fits typed evidence envelopes, or ranged retrieval).
- HARDEN-9's shortening predicate compares the persisted `Observation` with the persisted `Result.Output`
  (`Observation.StartsWith(Output, Ordinal)`); if compaction or retrieval changes what is persisted in either field, the
  ADR-0014 amendment must keep that predicate meaningful or replace it explicitly.

## Architecture constraints

- Never destroy audit or persisted evidence; compaction affects only the model-facing context.
- Compaction text is runtime-authored and delimited; tool output stays data.
- The core names no package or tool.

## Architecture gate for implementation (Proposed — architecture corrected after independent review; delta review pending)

The [ADR-0014 amendment](../../docs/architecture/adr/0014-explicit-planning-and-replanning.md#harden-8-amendment--context-and-budget-economy-proposed-architecture-corrected-after-independent-review-delta-review-pending)
is the normative implementation contract after delta review and acceptance. In particular:

- Derive `ev1:<task-guid-N-lowercase>:<persisted-step-index>`; validate the task id against the
  current task. HARDEN-9 digest step index `i` maps to the id ending in `:i`.
- Rebuild model-facing history on every step and resume: K=3 recent verbatim steps, M=12 older
  compact records of at most 256 units, and one 512-unit typed archive summary for everything
  earlier. Headers have a fixed 128-unit cap. Replan uses the current plan only and the directly
  triggering step verbatim, including at K=0, without duplication.
- `EvidenceRead/v1` is an exact internal text directive, not a provider tool call or operational
  tool. Native and OpenAI-compatible fallback modes preserve it before final/plan validation.
  Its `source` is explicit: `result` reads full persisted `Result.Output`; `observation` reads
  persisted `Observation`. Missing/null is unavailable, no silent fallback. Four requests per
  logical call, including malformed/rejected ones; fifth terminates. Audit each request as
  metadata through the additive `EvidenceReadAuditEvent`. Preserve evidence source fields.
- Persist the additive typed nullable `PlanStep.VerificationStatus`; compact `v=Refuted` derives
  only from it, never free-form Observation detail. Legacy null means unknown.
- ADR-0042's E1–E8 rule and persisted-step digest still apply on every relevant model call;
  compaction is not an evidence limitation, and retrieval never suppresses a limitation.
- Retry a `ModelFailureKind.ContextOverflow` once with K=0 only if compactable history exists.
  Initial-plan overflow ends without retry. The logical call stays aggressive through its
  continuations; the next normal call returns to configured K. Each attempt is audited and
  known usage counted.
- Validate `VerbatimHistorySteps` in 0–15, positive non-null `MaxTotalTokens` (350,000 default),
  and positive non-null `MaxAttemptDuration` up to 24 hours (60-minute default); null is an
  explicit operator opt-out for either nullable budget. No clamping. Duration counts active
  work, excludes human approval wait, and has a distinct cancellation signal and audited
  interruption outcome. Caller cancellation, ordinary timeout and rejection retain their own
  semantics.
- A model response crossing the lifetime token cap is persisted in one synthetic non-tool step
  at the next index with its `ModelCalls` and bounded final text, then ends
  `BudgetExceeded/TokenBudget`. No final acceptance, tool, read, retry or replan follows.

**E2E-13 measurement.** Script 40 steps over 15 + 15 + 10 accepted attempts with large
deterministic observations. Measure pre-provider `ModelRequest` components at steps 10, 20, 40.
For both a normal step and a replan, assert the **same** historical maximum at steps 10, 20 and
40: `K*4500 + M*256 + 512 + 128 = 17,212` UTF-16 units at default K=3, M=12. At K=0, the
replan trigger is a separate at-most-4,500-unit current turn; old history is at most 3,712.
Report fixed prompt/tool-schema cost, HARDEN-9 cost (at most 6,608), and bounded read
continuations separately. Range-read an archived old result beyond character 4,000 after resume;
assert persisted output/observations unchanged and HARDEN-9 still sees their limitations. Cover
both explicit sources, verification Refuted, rejected reads/audit, unique ids, malformed native
and fallback directives, fifth-read limit, duration interruption, initial-plan overflow, one
aggressive retry, and token-crossing persistence. No live provider is required.

Architecture self-check before implementation: persisted evidence destruction **NO**;
unbounded historical observations **NO**; old and cut-off output retrievable **YES**;
cross-task retrieval **NO**; arbitrary datastore access **NO**; more than one overflow retry
**NO**; lifetime token reset **NO**; HARDEN-9 digest regression **NO**; schema filtering **NO**.
