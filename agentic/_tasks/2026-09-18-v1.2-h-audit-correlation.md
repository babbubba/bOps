# V1.2-H — audit correlation and evidence provenance

Status: **planned**
Recommended model effort: **alto**
Repository: `bOps`
Depends on: V1.2-G complete
Governing ADR: `docs/architecture/adr/0030-privilege-reducing-multi-agent-delegation.md`

## Outcome

Complete the audit trail of a delegated objective (ADR-0030 section 8).

## Checklist

- [ ] Failing tests first: every event of a delegated run carries the correlation block; non-delegated runs are unchanged.
- [ ] Emit the new delegation events for every transition, including denial, rejection, cancellation and reconciliation.
- [ ] Envelope contents are audited as a hash plus reduced dimensions; arguments and output keep existing redaction (S6) and stay out of telemetry.
- [ ] `bops audit verify` still validates the chain across old and new events.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] The whole delegation chain and evidence provenance can be reconstructed from the local audit log.
- [ ] No secret or tool output reaches telemetry.
