# DOC-02 — Effective rules, amendment chains and contradictions

Non-normative working artifact of the DOC-02 phase of `agentic/_plans/ADR-consolidation-2026-10-22.md`. It is migration evidence, not current documentation, not a contract, and must not be loaded by the bootstrap.

## 1. Method

Status and evidence decide, never date or file name (plan §3.1). For each chain the base rule, the amending source, the resulting effective rule and where it lives (rule IDs of the five contracts) are recorded. A conflict that the evidence (an explicit supersession statement in a later Accepted ADR, or the code) resolves is recorded as *Resolved*; a conflict that does not resolve goes to REVIEW and its transfer is blocked. Decision status and implementation status stay independent; implementation evidence is in the contracts and in `DOC-02-traceability-matrix.tsv`.
